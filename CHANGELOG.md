# Changelog

## 1.0.1
- The mod list (Mod Menu) now links to the source code and the issue tracker.
- On a Minecraft version other than 1.21.1 the game now says so clearly at launch instead of crashing.

## 1.0.0
First public release!
- Remnants: your death rises as an echo of you, wearing your gear and holding your XP
- Path of the Soul: five ranks of permanent gifts
- Forgotten Graves, Forgotten Remnants (Drowned / Frozen / Sandy), Burial Caskets, Epitaphs
- The Ossuary crypts: Crypt Warden, Grave Mimic, Keener; the Ruined Belfry and the Night of Memory
- The Library and the Archivist
- The Soul King and his four-form Arsenal
- Every boss has its own boss bar and music theme
- Soul crafting: Soul Forge, Soul Crucible, Reflecting Pool, Soul Anchor, Phylactery and more
- The Soul Codex in-game guide
- English and Russian
